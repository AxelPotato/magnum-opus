# Goals of the method

The reasons people give for taking up the method, as most of them explain it to themselves. Written by Alex (2026-10-12). One line each; they are meant to be read as a set, and most people recognise themselves in a few of them.

People come to:

- Know why we exist, how the world works, and why it is the way it is.
- Find inner happiness and calm, and a point of support that holds steady whatever happens around them.
- Be a positive effect on their surroundings, their community and the world, starting with the people closest to them, and be respected and loved by them.
- Experience life without the filter of the mind, and see it for what it is.
- Obtain freedom from mental programs, traumas and negative thoughts.
- Realize their full potential, to its maximum, which means becoming the creator and master of their own life instead of being moved around by it, with real results and more energy in work and life.
- Evolve empathy, manage emotions, improve relationships and open their heart.
