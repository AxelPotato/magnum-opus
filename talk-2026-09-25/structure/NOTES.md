## 01-y-axis-letting-go

PART 1 · 0:00–4:10 · LETTING GO · the axis is on screen while you explain it
- Two axes of development. Start with the one less spoken about.
- Letting go = being at ease with yourself. Able to show your inner world. Knowing how much you believe your own fears, how much the ideas in your head run you, how much other people's opinions move you. Your internal resilience. The caring, supportive part of you.
- The ideas in our head are a source of tension, and the tension lives in the body. Health. And effectiveness: TENSION IS A TAX ON YOUR ABILITY TO SEE SOLUTIONS. With enough fears you never even look at the unusual solution.
- So: the more we let go, the more of life we see, feel and express.
- Credit Jonny Miller, "How to Unclench", once. Then: "the entire talk is one big graph. Congratulations. The most rational approach to spirituality I've ever tried."
- Clear the confusion: this axis is not just meditation. It's admitting you were wrong and taking the first step. Sorry, for real. The hard conversation. Feel the "I don't want to" and step anyway.
- Optional: the fist demo, if the room is cold.
- "Any questions on this one?"

## 02-x-axis-agency
