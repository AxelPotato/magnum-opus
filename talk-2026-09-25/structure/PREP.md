## Q&A · likely questions · one beat each

1. **“Isn't this just Stoicism?”** Yes, on the action leg and on presence. The addition: the letting-go leg trained in the body, daily. The Stoics wrote at night. This sits in the morning. Both.
2. **“How is it different from therapy?”** Not against it. Welwood's prescription is both. Therapy works the story. Stillness works the brace in the body. Inner agency is the act that follows. Many people do both.
3. **“What's the evidence?”** The two questions, answered today and in two weeks. The four observables. I won't quote studies for this method. The honest limit: the evidence is people, and you can meet them.
4. **“What is the school? Is it a cult?”** Name it plainly: the school, the teacher, twenty years, two centres, 120 people living it. Nothing to hide. The test for any school: do people leave more capable in the world, or less? Ask the ones who left.
5. **“Can I do this alone with an app?”** Yes, slowly, and you can fool yourself for years. The three dampers: a reference point, people further along, a guide and a schedule. An app gives the third, at best.
6. **“Doesn't ambition need tension?”** Biles. The drive got the medals; the tension took her out of the air in Tokyo. 95 × 2 = 190. The drive stays. The brace goes.
7. **“Where is this? Why there?”** Where the school grew up. Say the places flat. If that's a blocker for someone, it's a fair one.
8. **“What actually happens on the retreat?”** One paragraph: morning stillness, real work with real people, stillness again, for days. Everything else in the follow-up message.
9. **“Where does ADHD fit?”** Wide aperture, no steering wheel. Letting go widens what you see. Action is the wheel. Same chart.
10. **“Isn't letting go just passive? Isn't that the bypassing you described?”** That's the top-left. Letting go here is the apology, the call, the sentence. The most active thing you'll do this week.
11. **“Which room are you in?”** [YOUR SLOT. Say it honestly. The whole talk depends on this answer being true.]
12. **“Can you send the slides?”** Yes: the PDF. Say the group gets it.
