# Stay in contact

Thank you for reading all the way here. Before you go, a few last words from me, about the things people usually ask.

My friends and my family joked a lot at the beginning that this was a cult. None of them were super surprised by where I ended up, because I'd been talking to them about spirituality for about eight years before I even met Genadiy, so to them it was a natural next step. I think almost every one of us has heard some version of that joke from a relative or a friend: you moved to God knows where for some meditation stuff, so it's probably a cult. To be fair, I did move, and there is a lot of meditation. If it is a cult, it's a badly run one. I've just written a whole book without inviting you to join it.

That last part is serious. I'm not inviting anybody to become a student of Genadiy. If you'd like to try the practices, Giving School is the way we, his students, share our experience, his knowledge, the practices and the method with anyone who wants them. Some of it is online and some of it is retreats, and you take part as much as you want. I'm one of its instructors, and I've dedicated my time to making it more available in English.

And when someone who came closer decides it isn't for them, nothing special happens. We're sad when someone goes. Usually they've simply decided this isn't the life they want, or not right now, and most of them have very good friends here, so they stay in touch. Some go and do something else, maybe a project of their own, and we stay friends. Some come back. Very rarely someone leaves with bad feelings. I don't think it has happened in the last few years, but I do remember cases from before.

If you'd like to hear about workshops or online events I lead in the future, or you just have a question you'd like to ask me, feel free to leave your details below. They come straight to me, and I'll do my best to reply within a few days.
