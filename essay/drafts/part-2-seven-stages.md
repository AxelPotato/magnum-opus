# Part two: the seven steps and how each follows from the last

Read from `essay/part-2-spiritual.md` on branch `claude/project-thread-bk8z1r` (v3.4.2; step 4 updated for v3.5.0).

**The base: the axiom.** Three clauses: we are one; the whole experiences separation through individual lives; the separation ends. The computer picture (programs with private memory, one machine underneath) carries all seven steps.

## 1. Separation is a way of looking
If everything is one and the whole experiences separation, then separation can't be a fact about what exists. It is a fact about how the whole looks at itself. It is real as an experience and misleading as a final description, and that is all "illusion" means here. The two windows on the screen really are apart, but the machine has never heard of the distance. The physics passage sits here: quantum fields (electrons as ripples in one field), Bell's tests, and string theory as a hint, not a proof.
**Stems from:** clauses one and two of the axiom, directly.

## 2. The wall shows in the body
Knowing a view is a view doesn't remove it, so what keeps the separation in place? The rubber hand experiment shows the felt border of the self can be edited. The fist demo shows the wall as tension: clench, and you become a small hard object among others. Loosen, and the sentence goes soft. This is Part one's BE.
**Stems from:** step 1 (the view is held somehow) plus an observation, flagged as such, that the body is the machine's hardware.

## 3. One life is too short
The third clause says the separation ends, for each of us. But by Part one's 95 percent guess, most people never get there, and even the top right corner is only the start of the next map. So either the process fails for most, which contradicts clause three, or it takes more than one life.
**Stems from:** clause three plus Part one's numbers, and step 2 (the wall is held for a whole life without being noticed).

## 4. Something carries over
If the run continues, something carries what was learned. In the traditions that use the word, that's the soul; in the machine it's the saved state, not the session. What gets saved is the quintessence of the experience, not the whole run, and it accumulates from life to life until the soul is ready for the next step. Not remembering past lives is what the walls predict. The Advaita answer to "who carries over if there is one mind" is two levels: a saved state for the process, a single witness for the processor. Added assumption, named in the text: what continues is a thread of this particular run, gathering the essence of each life.
**Stems from:** step 3 (multiple lives) and the machine picture.

## 5. Karma, or the fingers
If we are one, everyone I could harm is part of the whole I belong to, like fingers hurting the hand. With the thread from step 4: I harm someone and call them other, the thread keeps going until it sees the whole, and by then it has stood in the place of everyone it called other. What I did comes back to me as the one standing there. The cheap version is blame; the version that follows is responsibility.
**Stems from:** clause one (no one else) and step 4 (the thread that returns).

## 6. Letting go, and repair
What undoes the wall and the harm? Punishment would be one more finger hitting another. Repair does it, plus letting go: Part one's inner agency (the sorry, the first step in a conflict) and loosening the body from step 2. You can't argue someone out of separation, because the body holds it as tension and needs the experience of letting go.
**Stems from:** step 2 (the wall is in the body) and step 5 (the harm to repair).

## 7. The return, in stages
Put the chain together and you get a path: loosen the wall, repair the fingers, and over many lives return to seeing the whole as one. It is gradual and it ends. The small square (all four rooms) is a separate self running as well as it can; the big square begins when separation itself is questioned. The arrow of consciousness runs from -4 to +4, pictured as speed against gravity (running, flying, orbit, escape velocity at the top right corner, then Sun, galaxy, universe), and after +4 comes "birth".
**Stems from:** all of the above: step 3 (many lives), 6 (what moves you), and the axiom's third clause (it ends). Part one's chart is re-read through it.
