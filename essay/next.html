If you’ve read the full essay and feel like taking part in any workshop or online session, you’re welcome to leave your details below. The online sessions I have in mind are in English, and each takes one subject, with some practical elements and answers to interesting questions. One lasts about an hour and a half, and anyone can stay longer when the discussion is good. You’re welcome to come to just one and see how it feels. Your details come straight to me, and I’ll answer you myself and try to keep you updated.

We, the students of Genadiy, practice together in two centers, one in Riga, Latvia, and one in Sochi, Russia, with over 120 people between them.

What I’m working toward now is a dream, so please read it as one. I call it an international center of human development.

I picture a group of people from many countries living and working in the same place who want to remember who they really are, the thing Part two says we forget, and to give that back to the world while they keep growing. A big part of the dream is working together to create new technologies, culture and projects, and new ways to make our world a better place. Everything in Part three would happen there every day as ordinary life. The place would be built around the people who live there. They would come because this has become what they want to do with their life, and some would stay for years while others moved on when life took them elsewhere.

It doesn’t exist yet. It’s the next step I hope we get to take, and I’d love to help build it.
