# Part zero: Before you start

I do everything I do, and I wrote this book, because looking back, the day I started my spiritual journey of inner evolution was the best day of my life. I know there are others like me who are searching for answers. If this book, or anything I ever did, becomes that moment for someone else, then I have fulfilled my mission in this life.

I'm Alex. I work in cyber security, and my spiritual search has run for more than fourteen years. In many ways I'm a modern monk with a day job.

If you've ever seen a monk, Buddhist or Christian, someone who took the serious spiritual path in one of the religions, and thought to yourself, I want that too, minus the religion: that's pretty much what I thought and felt when I chose this life.

Many people keep spirituality in the nice-to-have drawer, for a quiet weekend, a little abstract and far from real life. To me it's the foundation every human life stands on, and plain logic and common sense are enough to see why. Clear thinking and spirituality belong together, and this book is my attempt to show that, from three sides: rational, spiritual and practical. It says plainly what I've lived and what I believe, and you're free to disagree with any of it. Use whatever holds up in your own life and put the rest down.
